## 3. Findings (ranked; IDs refer to the test cases)

### A. Money the client sees
1. **Lines don't add up by 1¢** on estimates, invoices and the client page in about 1 of 3 jobs
   (e.g. C08: Subtotal $168.66 + Tax $13.91 + Card fee $5.29 = $187.86, but Total says $187.87).
   Each line is rounded on its own while the total comes from the unrounded numbers.
2. **Sales tax shown 1¢ low** on ~12% of round-dollar jobs (R02: $130 → tax $10.725 shows $10.72,
   should be $10.73). The app rounds three different ways (PDFs, client page, card charge), so the
   same item showed $0.17 on the PDF and $0.18 on the client page (C10), and the minimum-charge
   line showed $14.82 instead of $14.83.
3. **The invoice never shows payments or balance due** — after a deposit, or when paid in full, it
   still shows only the full total. Only the receipt shows what was paid (L1).

### B. Profit and Reports
4. **Re-pricing a saved job doesn't update its costs**: costs are recorded on the first save only
   (S02: 4 → 8 decals, price doubled, materials cost stayed $11.76; L3: added an item, same).
   Profit in Sales/Reports is wrong until the cost is edited by hand.
5. **Costs land in the month the job was priced, money in the month it was paid** (S15): a job
   priced last month and paid today shows this month's income with $0 cost and last month's cost
   with no income.
6. **Deleting a job deletes its payments**, so money already collected (and its sales tax)
   disappears from Reports and the tax report after the fact (S13). A "cancel/archive" would keep
   the history.
7. **Overpayments and refunds**: paying more than the balance is accepted with no warning; there's
   no way to record a refund (payments must be above $0); after lowering a paid job's price,
   nothing shows the money owed back — balance just reads $0 (S08, L6).

### C. Easy quoting mistakes the tool doesn't catch
8. **An item with no height (or width) saves as a $0 item with no warning** (S12); same for a
   negative size, and a discount bigger than the job silently makes it $0 (C13, R11).
9. **Reopening an old job after a rate change shows the new price without saying so**; Save
   re-prices it (and asks the client to accept again if they had) (S11).
10. **Install suggestion says "~0 min" for small items** (S09: 4 sqft at 100 sqft/hr); "Apply" sets
    install to $0.

### D. What the tool can't price well yet (needs owner decisions)
11. **No labor in item prices**: price = material × markup, plus one 15-min setup per job. With
    the built-in rates: cut lettering $1.20/sqft (C14: lettering for both truck doors, 36×14 each,
    = $8.40, tailgate = $3.20; the $96.46 job is mostly the $65 install), banner $0.92/sqft,
    25 yard signs ≈ $9.08 each, 500 3×3 stickers $76.56 by material vs $225 at $0.45 each.
    Weeding/masking/cutting time isn't priced anywhere except setup, design or install.
12. **Customer's own material**: print-only = $0.38/sqft ($26.25 for 70 sqft), cut-only = $0.00
    (R05, R06). Workaround today: minimum charge (C09, $175) or a flat price. Ties in with the
    parked "customer supplied material" idea.
13. **Card fee doesn't cover Stripe's full fee**: 2.9% vs Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢ per charge (and the
    fee is charged on the fee). Deposit + balance by card: the shop covers ~$0.62 (on $28),
    $0.80 (on $244), $2.53 (on $2,365). Also, cash/check clients pay the 2.9% too unless the box is
    unticked (owner's earlier choice — L1's client paid the fee though the balance was cash).
14. **A $0 job can't be accepted online** (known, skipped earlier) (R04/L5).

### E. Looks
15. Amounts of $1,000+ have no comma on PDFs and in the Dashboard ("$4976.09"); the client page
    shows "$4,976.09".
16. Subcontractor charges appear as "Hardware & Misc" on the client's estimate (R17).
17. The estimate PDF shows only the client's name (no address/phone); the invoice has them.
18. Work order: "Katy, TX, 77494" (extra comma); a plain board item reads
    "Media: None (substrate only) (not printed)".

### For your accountant (not a bug)
- Sales tax is charged on design, install and setup, and not on the card fee. Worth confirming
  that's right for Texas.

phone and computer layouts.

## 5. Monuments and channel letters (far future)
Can be done; nothing in the current design blocks it.
- **Today**: quote them as "Flat / Unit Price" items from vendor quotes (e.g. 8 letters × $180),
  raceway, permit, engineering and electrical as flat items, install as flat/subcontracted with your
  cost. Tested (R16 channel letters $3,397.37, R17 monument $4,976.09): totals, deposit, client
  acceptance and payments all work.
- **To do it properly later**: a per-letter / per-inch-of-height pricing mode for channel letters;
  saved "assemblies" (a monument = base + cabinet + faces + lighting + footing + install); payments
  in stages (e.g. 50 / 40 / 10) instead of only deposit + balance; per-item tax handling for
  permits/engineering (check with the accountant); probably bank transfer for large jobs (2.9% of
  $5,000 is $145).
